Examples

Four worked examples on simulated data. Open one to explore the dashboard. Each comes with the Python code that produced it.

  1. 01 Stress response in wearable data Repeated measures in three states: cleaning rules, distributions, one line per participant and a confidence interval. Open dashboard
  2. 02 Air quality: gaps, sensor faults and an episode Three stations, hourly readings. Detects spikes and a stuck sensor, finds a network-wide episode, compares stations and weekday rhythms. Open dashboard
  3. 03 Reaction time in two groups Comparing groups without overstating the evidence: bootstrap interval, permutation test, effect size, change within the session. Open dashboard
  4. 04 Survey data: careless answers, reverse scoring, reliability A questionnaire with negatively worded items. Removes straight-lining, reverse-scores, checks reliability with Cronbach's alpha and compares two groups. Open dashboard

What I do

Cleaning and checking

I load exports from devices, spreadsheets and databases, look for missing values, duplicates and impossible readings, and write down every rule I apply so you can disagree with it.

Analysis

Descriptive statistics, group comparisons, reliability checks and plots in Python with pandas. If your question needs a method I don’t use every day, I will tell you before we start.

Dashboards and figures

Interactive pages that open in any browser without installing anything, and static figures for papers and slides.

Software for your study

If the research needs its own program, such as an experiment or data-collection app, a processing pipeline or an internal tool, I can design and build it. We agree on scope and price first.

How a project works

  1. You send the question and a sample of the data, or a description of it.
  2. I reply with what I can answer and a fixed price for a small pilot.
  3. I deliver the code, the cleaned data and a short write-up, so you can re-run everything yourself.
  4. If it fits, we continue with the full data.

Confidentiality and authorship are agreed in writing before I open your data.

About

I’m Alexey Terletskiy, a PhD student in systems analysis. I work with all kinds of tabular and time-series data: experiments, sensors, surveys, logs and instrument measurements. I build the tools around it too: data collection in Python, databases, dashboards and small research programs.
