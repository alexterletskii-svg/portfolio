Stress response in wearable data. Simulated: a 12 ms drop was built in, so this shows the method, not a finding.

  • Cleaning removed 45 of 1200 windows (3.8%): 36 with missing heart rate and 9 with RMSSD above 250 ms (sensor artefacts).
  • Mean RMSSD: 53.0 ms at baseline, 41.2 ms under stress, 49.1 ms in recovery.
  • Change from baseline to stress: -11.8 ms (95% bootstrap interval -12.2 to -11.4, n = 40, dz = -9.6). RMSSD fell in 40 of 40 participants.
  • Heart rate rose by 7.7 bpm on average; in recovery RMSSD is still 3.9 ms below baseline.
