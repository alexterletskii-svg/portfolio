Reaction time in two groups. Simulated: the effect was built in, so this shows the method, not a finding.

  • Cleaning dropped 78 of 3600 trials (2.2%): anticipations under 150 ms and lapses over 1500 ms.
  • Participant medians differ by 89 ms (95% bootstrap interval 62 to 115); permutation test p < 0.001; Cohen's d = 1.6.
  • The fatigue group also slows down within the session: +15 ms per block of 10 trials, against -1 ms in the control group.
  • One median per participant keeps trials from the same person from counting as independent evidence.
